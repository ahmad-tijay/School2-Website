Academic Levels

We offer comprehensive secondary-level Islamic and Arabic education designed to build robust foundational and advanced knowledge.

JS

Junior Secondary (JS1 - JS3)

Foundation studies in Arabic language, basic Tajweed, simplified Fiqh, essential Aqeedah, and foundational Islamic history.

SS

Senior Secondary (SS1 - SS3)

Advanced specialization in Arabic grammar (Nahw/Sarf), Usul Fiqh, Mustalahul Hadith, Tafsir, and Literature (Adab & Balaagah).
